I'm Emir, an international student who likes figuring things out.

I grew up in Kyrgyzstan and moved to Los Angeles to study Computer Science at Los Angeles City College. Living and studying in a new country has taught me to be independent, disciplined, and comfortable learning things on my own.

I enjoy software development because it rewards patience and clear thinking. I work with C++, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS and React, and I'm most interested in problem-solving and in creating digital experiences that are genuinely useful.

I'm also curious about business, management and analytics, and how technology helps people make better decisions. I want to build practical skills that connect both worlds.
